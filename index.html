📖 Grading Scale & Passing Rule

  • A+ 90% – 100% Outstanding
  • A 80% – 89% Excellent
  • B 70% – 79% Very Good
  • C 60% – 69% Good
  • D 35% – 59% Satisfactory
  • F Below 35% Fail
📌 Passing Rule: A student is awarded PASS only if every individual subject has at least 35 marks. Failing any single subject marks the overall status as FAIL with Grade F.
